Questions

Things people usually ask.

What exactly does Brand OS do?
It gives you one clear idea to post about your business every day, written in your own words. You can also ask it questions, check what's trending, and turn comments into new ideas.
Do I need to know anything about AI to use it?
No. You don't need to know how AI works at all. You just answer a few simple questions once, and the app handles the rest. If you can send a WhatsApp message, you can use Brand OS.
Will my posts actually sound like me, or like a robot?
Like you. The app learns how you talk from the questions you answer and any real posts you add. It's built to avoid the stiff, robotic sentences most AI tools produce.
Is it free?
Yes, you can start for free — no credit card needed. Free accounts get a set number of ideas and drafts each day. If you need more, you can upgrade any time.
Do I have to post every single day?
No. Post as often as works for you. The app will still give you a fresh idea every day, but it's completely fine to only use it 2 or 3 times a week.
What if I don't know what to post about?
That's the whole point of the app. Open it, and it already has an idea waiting for you, along with a reason why it matters right now. You never start from a blank page.
Do you write the posts for me, or do I still have to write them?
The app writes a full first draft for you. You can post it as-is, tweak a few words, or rewrite it completely — whatever's fastest for you that day.
Can I use it on my phone?
Yes. Brand OS works right in your phone's browser — no app store download needed. You can even add it to your home screen so it opens like a normal app.
Is my information safe?
Yes. Your account is private to you — no one else can see your drafts, your posts, or how you answered your setup questions. Only you can access your own account.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. There's no contract and nothing auto-renews without your say-so. If you're on a paid plan, it simply stays active until the date you paid for, then quietly goes back to the free plan.
